Meri jaan,

Mujhe nahi pata ki ye letter padhne ke baad tum kya feel karogi, ya tum mujhe maaf kar paogi ya nahi. But kuch baatein hain jo main tumse saamne se shayad kabhi properly nahi keh paaya, isliye aaj dil se likh raha hoon.

I am really, really sorry. Maine tumhe hurt kiya. Mere gusse, mere words aur mere behaviour ne tumhe itna rulaya, aur iska koi excuse nahi hai. Chahe main kitna bhi gusse mein tha, mujhe tumse us tarah baat karne ka koi haq nahi tha.

Sabse zyada regret mujhe is baat ka hai ki jab tumhe meri zarurat thi, main tumhare saath nahi tha. Tum shayad mujhe dhoondh rahi thi, meri presence chahti thi, aur main available nahi tha. I know that must have made you feel alone, especially when you needed me the most.

Main ye nahi keh raha ki "main aisa hi hoon" ya "mujhse galti ho gayi, bhool jao." Nahi. Mujhe apni galti samajhni hai aur usse change karna hai. Sirf tumhe wapas paane ke liye nahi, balki isliye kyunki tumhare saath aisa behave karna genuinely galat tha.

Tumne meri anger deserve nahi ki.
Tumne mere harsh words deserve nahi kiye.
Tumne mere absence deserve nahi kiye.
Aur sabse badhkar, tumhe kabhi ye feel nahi hona chahiye tha ki tum mere liye important nahi ho.

Sach kahun toh mujhe darr lag raha hai ki shayad maine tumhe itna hurt kar diya hai ki tum ab mujhse door jaana chahti ho. Aur agar tumhe mujhse door rehkar peace milta hai, toh main tumhari choice ko respect karunga.

Main tumse ye promise nahi karna chahta ki "main kabhi gussa nahi karunga"— kyunki sirf promise kar dena easy hai. Main promise karna chahta hoon ki main apne gusse ko handle karna seekhunga, bolne se pehle sochunga, aur tumhe hurt karke usse normal samajhne ki galti nahi karunga.

Tum meri life mein sirf meri girlfriend nahi ho. Tum woh insaan ho jiske saath maine bahut kuch feel kiya, share kiya aur imagine kiya hai. Isliye tumhe khone ka thought mujhe andar se tod raha hai.

But main tumhe guilt feel karwa ke rokna nahi chahta.

Agar tumhe time chahiye, I'll give you time.
Agar tumhe space chahiye, I'll give you space.
Agar tum mujhse baat karna chahti ho, main bina defend kiye tumhari har baat sununga.

Bas ek cheez chahta hoon—agar kabhi tumhe lage ki mujhe apni galti explain karne ka nahi, balki apni galti sudharne ka ek chance milna chahiye, toh main us chance ko words se nahi, apne actions se prove karna chahta hoon.

I'm sorry for every tear I caused.
I'm sorry for every moment when you needed me and I wasn't there.
I'm sorry for making you feel hurt by the person who was supposed to make you feel safe.

I love you. And I'm sorry that my actions didn't always make you feel loved.

Tum mere saath raho ya na raho, main genuinely hope karta hoon ki tum hamesha khush raho. ❤️

I'm sorry, truly.
